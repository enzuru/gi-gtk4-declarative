* 0.8.0
    - First release. Declarative libadwaita widgets on top of
      gi-gtk4-declarative: `IsBin` instances for the single-child
      widgets, the two toolbar-view slots, and a declarative
      `AdwTabView`.
    - Built as `GHC2024`, which needs GHC 9.10 or newer. A package that
      depends on this one picks its own edition.
